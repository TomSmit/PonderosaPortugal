## The quiet years

The miller has long gone. When the millstones stopped turning, the river simply kept flowing past, and for a while the quinta was quiet.

## Mirjam

Mirjam lived there for a long time. When she moved back to the Netherlands she did not give the quinta up, and that is how it became a Ponderosa place. She opened the door to young people from the Netherlands and from Portugal. The plan is that within two years Portuguese young people can live there all year round as well.

## A place that lives again

Where grain was once ground, young people now sit at a long table in the evening. There is a theatre and an outdoor kitchen by now, and further down the river house and the Pisão. That last name recalls the days when wool was worked along this river. For seven years a group has come every summer.

## Who we are

Mirjam is always there. With her go people from the Ponderosa who have lived like this with young people for years and know the quinta like their own home. Who exactly comes along depends on the group. Photos will follow, so you know who you will be sitting at the table with.

## Ponderosa Portugal and the Ponderosa in the Netherlands

For now we are one organisation, with the Ponderosa in the Netherlands as home base. The plan is for Ponderosa Portugal to stand on its own feet and for the two places to keep learning from each other.
