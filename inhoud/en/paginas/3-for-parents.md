titel: Six weeks far away
intro: Your child is going to spend six weeks on a farm in Portugal, and that is exciting for both of you. Here you can read what life is like there, so you can let your child go with peace of mind.
vragen:
- vraag: Can I reach my child?
  antwoord: Yes. Your child simply takes a phone along, and you also get the numbers of the people from the Ponderosa who go with the group. There is one fixed person for your child whom you can always call if you are worried. Whatever you want to discuss with your child alone stays between the two of you.
- vraag: Who goes along?
  antwoord: People from the Ponderosa who have lived like this with young people for years and who know the quinta like their own home. If five young people go, five adults go too, and day and night there is always someone nearby.
- vraag: What if my child gets ill?
  antwoord: A bout of flu is simply spent in bed, with tea and rest. The hospital is close by, in Guarda, and an ambulance can reach the farm. If it is something serious, we make sure your child can go home, although some young people would rather stay.
- vraag: What about forest fires?
  antwoord: Summers in Portugal are hot and there are forest fires. We follow them closely every year with apps that track the fire, and we are in touch with the mayor and the fire brigade. In fifteen years a fire came truly close once. We always choose the cautious side.
- vraag: How do I prepare my child?
  antwoord: In practical terms it is simple. Apply for a European Health Insurance Card (EHIC), take out travel insurance if you like, and beyond that it is like going camping. For what happens there, you cannot really prepare anyone. That is why we talk with you properly beforehand, to see whether your child is ready.
- vraag: What should my child bring?
  antwoord: Warm clothes and summer clothes, a sleeping bag, a head torch or torch and a phone charger. A camping stove or air mattress is not needed, those are already there. Better too little than too much, and some money for souvenirs is handy.
- vraag: Can I visit?
  antwoord: We would rather you did not. The strength of these weeks lies in your child being somewhere completely different. Settling in takes time, and a visit pulls your child back into the old. If you would like to collect your child at the end, you can, as long as you arrange it with us.
- vraag: What does it cost?
  antwoord: The municipality pays for the care if it approves the programme. Travel and accommodation you pay yourself, meaning the return flights and €20 per day for food and outings. We share the dates well in advance, so the tickets are still affordable.
- vraag: What if it does not work out?
  antwoord: There will probably be a moment when your child wants to go home. Running away is often the first thing that comes to mind when something is uncomfortable. That is exactly when we walk beside your child, and usually calm follows. If it really becomes too much, we look honestly, together with you, at what is right. If your child goes home early, we take them to Porto airport and you arrange the flight. So buy a ticket that can be changed.
- vraag: And once my child is home?
  antwoord: Then we visit once more to look back together. There is often laughter too, about how hard it sometimes was and how well it turned out in the end. The municipality receives a report on how it went.
