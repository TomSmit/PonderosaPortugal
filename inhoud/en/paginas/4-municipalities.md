intro: A programme abroad calls for certainty. Below you can read how the programme is arranged and funded.
feiten:
- wat: Legal framework
  waarde: Dutch Youth Act (Jeugdwet) up to 18, Social Support Act (Wmo) from 18
- wat: Product
  waarde: Respijtwonen, daily rate
- wat: Group
  waarde: up to 5 young people, 5 mentors
- wat: Support
  waarde: at least bachelor level, some master level, SKJ-registered
- wat: Quality
  waarde: HKZ-certified, member of the BVKZ
- wat: Applications
  waarde: up to three months before the start; for the summer group, until the end of April
vragen:
- vraag: Which young people is it suitable for?
  antwoord: For young people stuck in their home situation or daily routine, for example after dropping out of school, and who can carry the programme. It depends not on age but on resilience and insight. With a lot of self-pity the chance of success is small, although the programme can work precisely as a lever. That is why we screen every application thoroughly, and not every application is accepted.
- vraag: How is it funded?
  antwoord: 'Through our product Respijtwonen, under the Jeugdwet or the Wmo. The daily rate covers the care. Travel and accommodation are paid by the parents: the flights and a contribution of €20 per day.'
- vraag: How do application and intake work?
  antwoord: Applications close three months before the start, so the care plan can be assessed carefully. For the summer group that is the end of April, and the earlier the better. An intake follows soon after, in which we look together, in depth, at whether the programme fits.
- vraag: What about Jeugdhulp Buitenland?
  antwoord: We are in talks with Jeugdhulp Buitenland about joining. In Portugal we work to the same quality standards as in the Netherlands.
- vraag: Who supports the young people?
  antwoord: Each young person has a personal mentor, who is also the fixed contact for you and the parents. All mentors have at least a bachelor's degree and SKJ registration. They work in a rota of day shifts and a sleep-in night shift, so someone is always present and everyone gets enough rest.
- vraag: How are safety and medical care arranged?
  antwoord: We are in close contact with the local mayor and fire brigade. We follow forest fires closely every year and, if there is a threat, act on the advice of the local fire brigade. Each young person has a European Health Insurance Card, and the hospital is close by, in Guarda.
- vraag: How do you report?
  antwoord: The programme only starts once the care request has been approved. We agree with the consultant whether and how they are kept informed during the programme. Afterwards we evaluate with the consultant and the parents, and with the young person if they wish. You receive a full evaluation report.
- vraag: How is aftercare arranged?
  antwoord: 'If the young person already receives care elsewhere, we hand over to that provider or practitioner and keep in touch. Otherwise we provide aftercare together with the parents: after a month we look back together and discuss the patterns that surfaced.'
- vraag: How are quality and privacy arranged?
  antwoord: Every mentor has an up-to-date certificate of conduct (VOG). The complaints procedure and confidential adviser run through the Ponderosa in the Netherlands. The young people are covered by our organisation's insurance. A dedicated privacy statement for this site will follow.
- vraag: How do you know it works?
  antwoord: We have done this for years with young people from our own day programme, and we see them again afterwards. They often come back lighter and calmer, and take themselves less seriously. We also see that they have become more curious about the world. With young people from outside, we look back together after a month and stay in touch with any practitioner involved.
