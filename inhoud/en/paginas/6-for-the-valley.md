## The door is open

Everyone is welcome, even without an appointment. Walk down the path and join us when we eat, because there is plenty and we love it. You do not need to look for anyone in particular. Whoever you meet, one of the young people or one of us, will make you welcome.

## Taking part in village life

When there is a market or a party in the village, the young people come along and help wherever they can. That way they get to know the valley through the people who have lived here all their lives, and we are grateful for that.
